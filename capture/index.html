Where it saves you time and money

Reconstructing receipts and mileage at month end costs hours, and missed records cost deductions. Capturing them at the truck, at the job or when a payment arrives means fewer lost records and less cleanup for you or your bookkeeper.

Truck Bluetooth reminds you before the mileage record is forgotten

Link Sanctuary Platforms Business Capture to the Bluetooth in your trucks. When a configured truck connects as it starts, the phone can sound a reminder to photograph the odometer. The capture can include GPS position, a timestamp, and the short location or job description you enter.

Optional monthly double-entry ledger service

The core software does not require a monthly rental. For businesses that want the added service, an optional monthly subscription organizes your Sanctuary Platforms Business Capture records into a double-entry ledger, and the monthly ledger/export can be emailed to you, your accountant, or both.

Your records remain your records

Sanctuary Platforms Business Capture follows the same ownership principle as every Sanctuary Platforms product: you keep control of the files you create. Optional services add convenience without making continued rental the condition for accessing your own records.
