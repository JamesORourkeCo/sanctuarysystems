SANCTUARY PLATFORMS CAMERA
An optional Android camera for better site-photo information

Better orientation data starts at capture.

Sanctuary Platforms Camera is an optional Android camera app for Sanctuary Platforms Design, part of Sanctuary Platforms. It helps contractors take more consistent site photographs and gives Sanctuary Platforms Design extra information for photo registration and rendering. It is not required.

Optional, but useful when render accuracy matters

A normal phone camera still works. Sanctuary Platforms Camera adds live orientation guidance so you can correct the phone before capture and follow a repeatable field-photo process.

  • Shows left/right orientation feedback.
  • Shows forward/back orientation feedback.
  • Displays heading and orientation during capture.
  • Writes additional capture information into the JPEG.
  • Gives Sanctuary Platforms Design better source information for registration and rendering.

The JPEG carries more than the picture

Sanctuary Platforms Camera embeds additional capture metadata in the JPEG. Sanctuary Platforms Design uses it in the registration workflow, helping make rendered views of real site photographs more consistent and accurate.

A more repeatable capture workflow

Standardized capture reduces variation between employees and between visits to the same property, and it does not require the person taking the picture to become a camera or CAD specialist.
