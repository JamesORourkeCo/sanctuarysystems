You should not be held for ransom

Many design tools are rented by the month, and your own files can be locked behind the subscription. Many contractors also end up depending on one CAD-trained employee. When that person leaves, the design work stalls. Sanctuary Design is built so neither one controls your business.

What you save

No software rent

You buy the software per major release instead of paying a subscription forever.

You own your designs

Your files stay yours. Stopping a subscription is never the price of opening your own work.

No CAD-trained staff

Routine design work does not require CAD certification, so employees become productive far faster.

Easy to say yes to

Customers see their own property in a realistic rendered view and a clear proposal from the same design, so the decision is easier to make. They can watch the price update as the design changes.

One tool, not four

Design, proposal pricing and printable drawing sheets come from the same project, not separate programs.

Pricing is built in, so proposals come straight from the design

Materials and products are linked to pricing inside the project. Modify the design in front of the customer and see how the change affects the price, live, as they decide. When they are ready, produce the customer proposal and order list from the same data. Drawing sheets can be printed for inclusion in your building permit submissions.

Use more power only when you need it

Optional server rendering

If a job needs a GPU, use the server instead of buying a workstation. It is optional, never required.

Optional AI assistance

If a computer is not your comfort zone, AI can help with ideas, layouts and alternatives.

Not locked to one renderer

If AI rendering outpaces traditional rendering, you can use AI for the render instead of waiting on a software update.

Designed with future automation in mind

Machine-guided excavators and trucks that follow digital instructions already exist, and more autonomous equipment is coming to the job site. Sanctuary Design saves each project as a structured .arch file: measured layout, materials and quantities in one precise record. The goal is that when your equipment is ready to take instructions, your design data already is. You do the design work once, and the crews and machines that follow it work from the same instructions.

From measured plan to rendered view

This is not just a photorealistic picture. It is what is in your proposal, placed on a photograph of the customer's actual home, so they can see what a pergola or a tall tree will look like from their own view before they decide. Every object in the design stays a real object: ask Sanctuary Design to move a tree two feet to the left and it moves that tree two feet to the left. A flat AI-generated image usually cannot, because it does not know which pixels are the tree.

Exterior or interior. One workflow.

  • Exterior: landscapes, outdoor living, hardscape, structures, planting and site layout.
  • Interior: kitchens, bathrooms, furniture placement, cabinetry, fixtures, finishes and wall colors.
  • One color picker with the standard palette or the Benjamin Moore paint palette.
  • The Sanctuary product library, or supported manufacturer-supplied 3D model and CAD files.
  • Measured 2D planning and customer-facing 3D visualization in the same project.
  • Photo-based registration and rendering for real sites, with headless processing for unattended rendering.
